# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented here.

## [1.1.0] - 2026-08-06

### Added

- Ten Level 14 tricks
- Ten Level 15 tricks
- Level 14 and Level 15 filter options through the existing dynamic filter system
- Level 14 and Level 15 entries in the source spreadsheet
- Project README and deployment instructions

### Changed

- Expanded the finals trick pool through Level 15
- Moved the 30-point finals bonus from Level 11 to Level 14
- Moved the 50-point finals bonus from Level 12 to Level 15
- Updated results-screen labels for the new bonus levels
- Increased the complete trick list from 130 to 150 tricks

### Removed

- Additional finals bonuses for Levels 11 and 12

## [1.0.0]

- Initial Kendama World Cup 2026 practice app release
- Preliminary and finals practice modes
- Search and multi-level filtering
- Timed rounds and results scoring
